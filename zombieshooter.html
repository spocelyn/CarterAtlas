ZOMBIE SHOOTER

Pick your gun below for each character. Player 1: Shift to roll, Q for grenade, E for your class ability, F to loot. Player 2: Enter to roll, Ctrl for grenade, ' for ability, ; to loot. Find gas stations, bunkers, radio towers, hospitals and military outposts and hold your loot key nearby for ammo, armor, healing, or gear. Watch for yellow Boomers that explode when killed, and green Venom zombies that poison you. Every 5th wave is a BOSS WAVE, every 3rd brings a HORDE. Watch out for dust storms - they curve your bullets with the wind, blind you past a short range, and slow you down if you walk against the wind.
